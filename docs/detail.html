Reading the signs

Every value on this page, whether on the tree, on the tape, or at a terminal, is stated from the point of view of whoever is about to act. That is why each returned utility flips sign on the way back up: util[a] = −cfr(child). A terminal bp showing +1 means the player to act at that history collects one chip.

Why regret is measured against the baseline

The update is r[a] += π_opp · (u[a] − u_node), never π_opp · u[a]. Subtracting u_node is what turns a thin edge between two actions into the whole signal, and it is what makes the fixed point the indifference condition. Drop it and every information set sits near uniform forever.

Two different weights

strategy_sum is weighted by the acting player's own reach; regret_sum by the opponent's. The second is what "counterfactual" names: strip out your own probability of arriving, so you keep learning at the information sets your current strategy avoids.
